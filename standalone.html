💡 Real-World Architectural Analogy CONCEPTUAL ANALOGY

Use this mental model to immediately understand the physical design trade-offs:

👨‍🍳 Multicore = "One Kitchen, Multiple Chefs"

All chefs (cores) stand inside the same kitchen (silicon die). They share the same spice rack (L3 Cache) instantaneously with zero walking. It is fast, compact, and cheap. But if the kitchen catches fire (die overheat), every chef must evacuate (all cores crash).

🏢 Multiprocessor = "Separate Restaurant Branches"

Each restaurant (CPU socket) is an independent building. If Branch 2 burns down, Branch 1 stays open and serves customers (high fault tolerance). But whenever they need ingredients from the central supplier (Main Memory), supply trucks must travel over the city highway (System Bus & Arbiter), causing heavy traffic congestion.
